About this virtual QKD lab

QKD Physical-Layer Lab is a browser-based QKD simulator for studying how fibre loss, detector characteristics, WDM coexistence with classical channels (spontaneous Raman scattering) and multicore-fibre crosstalk affect the error rate (QBER) and secret key rate of quantum key distribution links. Choose a protocol, set the physical parameters, sweep any variable, and compare the model with your own measurements.

Which QKD protocols can I simulate?

Decoy-state BB84 (analytical lower-bound model, with an optional Gaussian finite-size approximation), coherent one way (COW, a phenomenological estimate) and measurement-device-independent QKD (MDI-QKD, a simplified asymptotic model).

What can I experiment with?

Single-mode and multicore fibre, G.652.D loss versus wavelength, per-channel classical power, wavelength and propagation direction, receiver filter bandwidth, InGaAs SPAD and SNSPD detector settings, dead time, afterpulsing, timing jitter, optimisation, sensitivity analysis, Monte Carlo checks and automated validation tests.

Is this a security proof?

No. It is an analytical physical-layer simulator for engineering studies, teaching and experiment planning. COW and MDI results are not security-grade, and the finite-size option is not a composable finite-key proof. Each model states its assumptions on the page; verify equations and references against the original papers before publication.
